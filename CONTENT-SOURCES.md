# Content design and references

All shipped questions, scenarios, solutions and parameter rules are original practice content. The website does not download questions at runtime, scrape a publisher, reproduce diagrams from books, or require you to upload questions. We use standard curriculum methods and the broad assessment formats seen in exams: definitions, explanation chains, numerical models, interpretation and evaluation.

## Numerical design

Each structure has constrained parameters and a mathematical solution. Some use selectable physical/chemical scenarios, and all randomised calculations use computed results. Parameters deliberately avoid invalid domains such as zero denominators, negative kinetic energies and exhausted buffer components. Reference constants and assumptions are printed in questions when needed. Displayed answers are rounded; accept correct equivalent working and sensible rounding.

## Conceptual design

Recall prompts test specific definitions or facts. Written explanations supply four to six distinct marking points and a complete example answer. These prompts are finite; many topic/type pairs currently contain one prompt. They recur for retrieval practice. A novel seed cannot create a new conceptual idea.

## Limits

Broad groups such as Further Mechanics 2 contain much more material than two structures. All of your selected broad groups have some practice, but the collection does not cover all subtopics, organic mechanisms, diagrams, practical skills, proofs, or exam-style combinations. FP1/FP2/Decision content is not expanded. Self-marked practice percentages are not predicted grades. Use actual papers and their official mark schemes periodically.
